How it works

  1. Buy a ticket for exactly 2 USDC on Base. You can hold up to 25 at a time.
  2. Your ticket never expires. It enters every weekly draw until it wins.
  3. Every Sunday at 17:00 UTC, one ticket wins 33% of the pool. That ticket is burned; the other 67% stays in the pool and keeps growing.
  4. The winner is picked with a random number from Pyth Entropy, delivered on-chain after the draw is requested. Nobody, including us, can choose or predict it.
  5. The prize is paid in the same transaction. The transaction hash is public and listed below.

Why not pay out everything?

Because tickets last forever. If the whole pool were paid out each week, older tickets would have nothing left to win from. The 67% that stays is what every ticket still in play is competing for.

The money in the pool belongs to no one until a draw moves it. The contract has no function that lets the owner withdraw it.

Rules

  • 1 ticket = 2.00 USDC, sent through the contract’s “buy tickets” function. Only USDC on Base.
  • Maximum 25 active tickets per wallet. Anything beyond that is refused.
  • Tickets bought before the draw is requested on Sunday 17:00 UTC are in that week’s draw. Later ones join the next week.
  • Anyone can send extra USDC to the pool with the “sponsor” function. Sponsors are listed on-chain; they get no tickets.
  • Up to 7% of each payout goes to the developer. The contract allows this share to be lowered, never raised.
  • Rules never change. If we ever want a different game, we open a new pool. This one keeps running exactly as written.
  • Send from a wallet you control, not from an exchange. Prizes go back to the address that bought the ticket.

Questions

Who runs the draw on Sunday?

Anyone can. The contract only allows the draw after 17:00 UTC; whoever calls it first triggers it. We call it ourselves if nobody else does.

Can the developer take the pool?

No. The owner can only lower the developer share, change the address that receives it, and withdraw the small amount of ETH kept for randomness fees. There is no function to move USDC out of the pool.

What are my odds?

Each ticket has one chance in the number of tickets in play, every week, until it wins. More tickets in play means a bigger pool and lower odds per ticket, like any lottery.

What if I send the wrong amount or the wrong token?

The contract only accepts whole tickets of 2 USDC. USDC sent directly to the address without using the buy function is added to the pool as a sponsorship and cannot be returned. Other tokens are lost.

Why do I need ETH too?

Base charges a tiny fee for every transaction, paid in ETH. A few cents’ worth is enough for several purchases.

This is a game of chance with a negative expected return. Most tickets never win. Play only with money you can afford to lose.

You must be 18 or older. Online lotteries are restricted or illegal in some countries and US states; it is your responsibility to know whether you may take part where you live. The pool grows only with new tickets; if nobody joins, the remaining pool is paid out over the following weeks.

Only 2 USD is an experiment. The contract is immutable; this website is just a window onto it.
